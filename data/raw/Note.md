## 3. Raw Data Taxonomy & Domain Layout
The raw dataset captures Q3 activity for 7,043 telecommunications customers in California across six functional domains:

1. **Identifiers:** `CustomerID`, `Count` (Administrative tracking).
2. **Location Metadata:** `Country`, `State`, `City`, `Zip Code`, `Lat Long`, `Latitude`, `Longitude`
3. **Demographics:** `Gender`, `Senior Citizen`, `Partner`, `Dependents`.
4. **Subscribed Services:** `Phone Service`, `Multiple Lines`, `Internet Service`, `Online Security`, `Online Backup`, `Device Protection`, `Tech Support`, `Streaming TV`, `Streaming Movies`.
5. **Billing & Financials:** `Tenure Months`, `Contract`, `Paperless Billing, Payment Method`, `Monthly Charge`, `Total Charges`
6. **Targets & Upstream Indicators:** `Churn Label`, `Churn Value`, `Churn Score`, `CLTV`, `Churn Reason`.

## 4. Known Raw Data Quirks & Preprocessing Directives

> [!WARNING]
> **Total Charges Whitespace Bug**
> Continuous numeric values in `Total Charges` are parsed as `object` (string) due to blank space strings (`" "`) for 11 new accounts with `Tenure Months == 0`.
> * **Mandatory Action:** Coerce using `pd.to_numeric(df['Total Charges'], errors='coerce')` and fill missing values with `0.0`.

> [!WARNING]
> **Target Leakage Safeguard**
> `Churn Reason` and `Churn Score` must be strictly excluded from the feature set prior to model training:
> * `Churn Reason`: Post-hoc qualitative feedback collected only after account cancellation.
> * `Churn Score`: Upstream model prediction generated by IBM SPSS Modeler.

* **Class Imbalance Profile:** Target exhibits ~26.54% churners (1,869 churned vs. 5,174 retained).
* **Administrative Pruning:** Non-predictive IDs and geographic variables (`CustomerID`, `Zip Code`, etc.) must be removed before model fitting.
